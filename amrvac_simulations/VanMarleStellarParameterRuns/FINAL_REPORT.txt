# Purpose: Record the tested repair, rejected alternatives, and limits before production restart.

Selected configuration
  flux_scheme=20*'tvdlf', limiter=20*'minmod', time_stepper='threestep'
  courantpar=0.1, qstretch_baselevel(1)=1.03
  Domain 0.01--20 pc, 192 base cells, AMR max level 3.
  The screenshot's cumulative phase ends remain 4.3, 4.5, and 4.77 Myr.
  MS: 1e-6 Msun/yr, 2000 km/s. RSG: 5e-5, 15. WR: 1e-5, 2000.
  Assumed wind temperatures: MS/WR 10000 K, RSG 1000 K (not specified in image).
  Ambient n_total=1e4 cm^-3, T=100 K. All four conduction/cooling switches retained.
  Exact SPEX_DM cooling and Tfix=100 K in cooling-enabled runs; saturated Spitzer
  conduction with STS in conduction-enabled runs. No physical temperature floor
  is added to the cooling-disabled controls.

Evidence for the selected setup
  tvdlf_physics_1000yr_*: all four physics combinations completed 1000 yr.
  tvdlf_phase_150yr_*: MS->RSG and RSG->WR in each combination, eight tests total.
  These transition tests switch at 50 yr and end at 150 yr; production ages do
  not change. They exercise abrupt source changes in young bubbles, not a
  physically evolved 4.3/4.5 Myr bubble.
  ALL 12 passed with finite native cell values, strictly positive recovered
  pressure, and the 100 K floor respected in cooling-enabled runs.
  ALL 12 recorded zero activations of the new numerical energy-rescue guard.
  Runtime logs confirm the intended activation of conduction and cooling.
  Validation is reproduced by validation_tools/summarize_validation.py --require-selected.

Why this configuration was selected
  The old absolute pressure floor could vanish in E-KE cancellation; the new
  guard uses a representable increment and explicitly rejects nonfinite states.
  A larger absolute floor fixes that rounding path, but does not resolve the
  strong-transition positivity problem by itself.
  HLL variants passed several MS tests but failed strong cooling-enabled WR
  transitions under the allowed correction limit. Reducing CFL to 0.05 or 0.025
  or disabling logarithmic reconstruction did not solve the difficult HLL test.
  TVDLF at CFL 0.1 passed it with zero energy rescues, then passed the full matrix.
  Moving the inner boundary to 0.05 pc changed the 1000-year bubble radius by
  about 17%, so that domain change was rejected.
  Gentler stretching keeps the physical boundaries and raises the minimum cell
  width. In the hydro-only 1000-year comparison, outer disturbed radius changed
  from 0.09456 to 0.09515 pc (about 0.63%). This is a screening comparison, not a
  spatial-convergence demonstration of density peaks or cooling-shell widths.

Protection retained for full runs
  Invalid/divide-by-zero/overflow traps enabled in the executable.
  Explicit finite checks before pressure conversion and after advancement.
  Refuse an individual energy rescue above 0.1% of the cell energy scale.
  Refuse the volume-weighted state-evaluation rescue budget above 0.1% of the
  analytic injected wind energy. The evaluation sum includes RK intermediate
  states and is not a measurement of net energy-conservation error.
  External supervisor stops all runs if any case exits unsuccessfully or logs
  NaN/Infinity. Successful completion requires independent native-cell auditing.

Production is restarted from fresh ambient initial conditions in this folder.
The invalid earlier campaign and all diagnostic evidence are preserved separately.
The full 4.77 Myr lifetime has not been validated to completion. TVDLF's extra
numerical diffusion and unresolved cooling layers require resolution/timestep
comparisons before using detailed shell widths or density peaks scientifically.
